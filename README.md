## Important
The CAC certificate identifies the current registered business name as:
ABDM GLOBAL COMPUTER TECHNOLOGY
Business Name Registration No. 9751406

The supplied business plan uses the older name "ABD COMPUTERS"; its service descriptions, vision, mission, market information and operating hours were used as source content while the current CAC name was used for the website branding.

Phone, WhatsApp and email details were not present in the supplied files, so no contact numbers or email addresses were invented.

## Publishing
Upload the contents of this folder to a static host such as your chosen web hosting provider. Before launch, add the business phone/WhatsApp/email and domain.
